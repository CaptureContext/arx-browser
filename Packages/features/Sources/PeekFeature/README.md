# PeekFeature

One contained page presentation with explicit source window, pane, tab, space, and profile identity. ViewerItemFeature owns navigation/error observation. Temporary previews ask before-unload before close or replacement; existing tabs return to their host without discarding their session. The parent validates ownership and commits same-session promotion only after opening succeeds.

The overlay exposes destination URL, identity, Close, Open as Tab, and temporary-session Open in Little Arx. Manual HTTP(S) location entry is available; native in-page link hit-testing and Force Click are not supplied by the current engine bridge. Existing permanent tabs cannot become temporary Little Arx sessions. Native keyboard, VoiceOver, focus restoration, popup/download/permission policy and renderer interaction remain device acceptance checks.

AppFeature hosts the overlay for main, additional normal and expanded Little Arx windows. Little sources capture their viewer generation and use the window ID as their single-pane identity. PeekURLButton supplies localized typed/pasted URL entry without native link targeting. It freezes the displayed source identity when opened and submits that identity for revalidation, so a profile switch while entering a URL cannot retarget the preview. Its shared history control captures the preview's tab ID, generation and native history snapshot for stale-selection rejection. Surface handoffs suspend observation and native attachment before moving ownership. Source removal requests close once; cancelling before-unload keeps the page available for manual close without repeating the prompt on unrelated actions. The overlay's Close control is available. The staged unhandled-Escape callback
accepts only while idle, then defers the same close action and rechecks Peek,
tab, viewer generation and native source. Before-unload remains authoritative.
The callback is shared by Main and Little hosts. Production composition remains
disabled until the owned native fixture passes; no Escape monitor runs ahead of
web content.

PeekView is a floating card using AppUI metrics: continuous 12-point corners, a hairline and a large soft shadow. Close, Open as Tab and Open in Little Arx sit beside the card's top-right corner as 36-point Arx buttons (`BrowserPeekControlStyle`, DEV-1224): filled rounded squares with continuous corners and no stroke, as Settings' buttons (DEV-743), on a solid backing so they read over any page, lighter when hovered, darker when pressed, with the accent ring under keyboard focus. Each shows its title as a hover hint after half a second. AppFeature places the card over the pane it was opened from, read from the pane's unscaled frame (`browserPeekPaneBounds`): it keeps the pane's height, leaves about 7% of the width free on each side, and puts the buttons in the right margin. The sidebar and other split panes stay uncovered. Little Arx has no viewer panes and keeps a card inset about 5% from each window edge. The source pane scales back to 96% and dims to 55% opacity (`browserRecedesBehindPeek`, `BrowserMetrics.Peek.backgroundOpacity`, DEV-1224), and restores when the preview starts closing. Only the pane dims: the card is drawn over it, not inside it, so the peeked page keeps its own colors (DEV-1173). While the Peek is open, the tab it came from shows the peeked site's favicon in the sidebar (`PeekFeature.State.peekedTab`, which AppFeature hands down as AppUI's `browserPeekedTab`): at the trailing end of its row or of its segment in a Split View row, and as a small badge over its Favorite tile's corner. A Peek not opened from a tab, or one being promoted or released, shows none. Clicking anywhere outside the card closes the preview through the same before-unload path as Close.

A Peek opened by a link click or Force Click keeps the pointer's screen position as `origin`. The card grows out of that point and shrinks back into it on close or replacement. Keyboard, menu, routing and typed-URL previews have no origin and appear and disappear at once. Promotion never shrinks the page toward the link. Reduced motion replaces the growth with a short crossfade and leaves the page behind unscaled; it still dims, as a crossfade. The typed-URL entry (`PeekURLButton`) uses Arx's button, text field and primary Preview button.
