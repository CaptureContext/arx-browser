# ChatFeatureTests

Every suite that uses dependencies, a store or `@Shared` takes `@Suite(.dependencies)` (DEV-970). Without it, every test in the process shares one set of dependency test values, including swift-sharing's storage and `@Shared` references. A test then reads what another left behind, so it passes alone but fails on a repeat or next to another.

Reducer tests for ChatFeature with `ChatWorld`: a fake provider that scripts a turn's events, failure or an endless run, an in-memory conversation store, fake browser access and a `TestClock` that moves with the date. Nothing touches the real home folder, a Claude account or a process.

They cover the entry (a Tab-started chat waits for the profile's defaults, then runs with or without browser tools), streaming into the reply, the 20-minute idle expiry and its restart after a follow-up, failures that keep the prompt and Try Again (signed out, expired sign-in, usage limit, usage credits, turn limit, browser tools, missing runtime) and partial answers, Stop and turns that keep running while the person looks elsewhere, unread answers, the panel's screens and Your Chats' filters, explicit attachments, Pin, Rename, Archive and Delete, per-chat models and providers, and the Settings defaults. App wiring is covered by `ChatEntryTests` in IntegrationFeatureTests.

`ChatAttachmentTests` cover what Return does under each Settings › Assistant choice, files, pasted images and captures joining the composer of the surface they came from, provider limits with their message, and turns that carry the bytes while the transcript keeps only a preview (DEV-798).

`ChatPanelPartsTests` cover Your Chats' filters and month sections and where the + menu lists the window's tab, folders and tabs. `ChatPanelSnapshotTests` (opt-in with `ARX_DESIGN_SNAPSHOTS=<dir>`) write the chat panel in light and dark: a new chat, a short answer, a long one with tool activity, a failure, and Your Chats; and the composer with one line, several lines and attachments, and capped in a wide chat tab (DEV-798).

`ChatMarkdownTests` (DEV-998) check the blocks an answer's Markdown becomes: headings, paragraphs and rules; inline styles, with only web links kept and images never loaded; nested lists with their numbers and task boxes; nested quotes; code blocks with their language; tables with their header, alignments and a cell for every column; and how a streaming answer's tail settles (open emphasis and code closed, innermost first, a table held until its delimiter row). DEV-1031 adds empty rows whose outer pipes or indentation differ from the rows above, and nested unfinished spans. `ChatMarkdownSnapshotTests` (opt-in with `ARX_DESIGN_SNAPSHOTS=<dir>`) write each element at the panel's width in light and dark, including a table wider than the panel and a streaming tail.
