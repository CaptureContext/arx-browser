# Glossary: German (de)

Translators of Arx into German use these terms. A term here wins over any other choice.

## Language rules

- Address: informal "du", as Apple's German macOS and its support pages do ("Du kannst jederzeit ändern …", "Wähle nur die geschützten Ordner …"). Use du, dich, dein, lowercase. Never "Sie". Verified: https://support.apple.com/de-de/149699 and https://support.apple.com/de-de/guide/safari/welcome/mac
- Capitalization: German noun capitalization always applies; otherwise sentence case. Menu items and buttons capitalize only the first word and nouns ("Neues privates Fenster", "Tab in ein neues Fenster bewegen").
- Compounds: follow Apple's spellings exactly: Tabgruppe (one word), Bild-in-Bild (hyphens), Festplattenvollzugriff, Standard-Webbrowser, Seitenleiste, Favoritenleiste. Join a coined English name with a hyphen: Arx-Assistent, Proxy-Link, Tab-Archiv.
- Verbs in buttons and menu items: infinitive at the end ("Tab schließen", "Lesezeichen hinzufügen"), never imperative.
- Show and hide: Apple uses "einblenden" and "ausblenden" for showing and hiding UI parts (Seitenleiste einblenden), "anzeigen" for showing content.
- Save: Apple's German uses "sichern" for saving files and documents (Sichern unter …); keep that in menus. "Speichern" is fine for storing a setting or data.
- Punctuation and ellipsis: Apple's German puts a space before the ellipsis character in menu items that open a dialog: "Drucken …", "Einstellungen …". Use the single character "…", never three dots.
- Quotation marks: „…“ (German low-high). Apple quotes UI names this way: „Ablage“ > „Neues privates Fenster“.
- Plural categories: one, other.
- macOS Spaces: Apple keeps the English word "Spaces" (Mission Control; "Verwenden mehrerer Spaces auf dem Mac"), glosses it once as "Bereich (Space)", and labels each one "Schreibtisch" in Mission Control. Verified: https://support.apple.com/de-de/guide/mac-help/mh14112/mac
- Arx space: "Raum" (plural "Räume"), because Apple already takes "Space", "Bereich" and "Schreibtisch" for macOS Spaces, and "Arbeitsbereich" would mean a workspace; "Raum" is short, native and free.
- Tab: der Tab, plural die Tabs (Apple's gender and plural).
- Length: German runs about 30 percent longer than English; prefer Apple's short forms (Tab, Seitenleiste) over descriptive phrases in buttons.
