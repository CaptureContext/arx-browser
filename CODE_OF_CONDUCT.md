# Code of Conduct

People care about their browser and disagree about what it should do, often and in public. That's fine. This page is about how we disagree.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people here are giving their free time.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Say what is wrong with something and why.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no, and that the reason may be taste.
- Help newcomers find their way. Everyone started without knowing where anything was.
- Keep other people's private details private: never post someone's email, logs, diagnostics, screenshots of their tabs or anything they shared with you in confidence.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexual language or images.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate derailing.
- Threats, and encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after a maintainer has asked you to stop.

This applies in issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, and anywhere you speak for the project.

## Reporting

- Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, issue or discussion (the "…" menu beside it). It reaches the repository's maintainers.
- To say something privately, use **Help → Send Feedback…** in Arx, kind **Other**, and start it with "Conduct report". It goes only to Arx's maintainer and is never published. Without Arx, open a private report under the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** the same way.

A maintainer reads every report, keeps the reporter's name private, and answers within a week. If a report concerns a maintainer, that person is left out of handling it.

## What happens

Depending on what happened: a private word, a public note on the thread, a temporary ban from the repository, or a permanent one. A maintainer may hide or delete comments and issues that break this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
